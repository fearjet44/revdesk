# Administration

This section describes how the Company controls this manual. The Director of Operations is responsible for the integrity of the issued revision.

## Currency

1. Review this manual at least annually, and whenever a regulation, authorization, or company procedure changes.
2. Open a numbered change for every amendment. Do not edit an issued section in place.
3. Issue a new revision only after the change has been reviewed and approved.
4. Distribute the issued revision to every person with a duty described in this manual.

:::caution
A printed excerpt is uncontrolled the moment it leaves the electronic master. Stamp the print time on any paper copy and destroy it when the duty is complete.
:::
